Do Duc Anh

PhD student · School of Computing and Information Systems, Singapore Management University

I am a first-year PhD student at Singapore Management University, advised by Asst. Prof. Linh Kästner in the ARENA Lab.

I build simulation and generation tools for robots that move among people: photorealistic simulators, pedestrian behaviour driven by natural language, and generated 3D worlds for training and benchmarking social navigation. I contribute to the open-source Arena-Rosnav platform.

Before SMU I was a research assistant at the National University of Singapore, and I received my B.Sc. in Information Technology from FPT University, Hanoi.
