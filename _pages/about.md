I am a first-year PhD student at [Singapore Management University](https://computing.smu.edu.sg/), advised by
[Asst. Prof. Linh Kästner](https://faculty.smu.edu.sg/profile/kaestner-doan-hoang-linh-8386) in the
[ARENA Lab](http://linhdoan.io/).

I build simulation and generation tools for robots that move among people: photorealistic simulators, pedestrian
behaviour driven by natural language, and generated 3D worlds for training and benchmarking social navigation. I
contribute to the open-source [Arena-Rosnav](https://github.com/Arena-Rosnav) platform.

Before SMU I was a research assistant at the National University of Singapore, and I received my B.Sc. in Information
Technology from FPT University, Hanoi.
